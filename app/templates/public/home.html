Why the system was created

A reliable path from request to timetable

Smart Class requests were previously delivered through handwritten letters. With no central record, it was difficult to confirm which requests had been received, reviewed, approved, or scheduled. A letter could be delayed, forgotten, or misplaced before reaching the final timetable.

The Smart Class Management System replaces that process with one organized digital workflow. Requests are recorded immediately, the Patron or Matron reviews them in one queue, and approved sessions are published clearly.

The goal is simple: make every request visible, traceable, and easier to manage.

How it works

Four clear stages

Each request follows the same visible process from submission to a public approved timetable.

1

Submit

A Teacher or Class Monitor enters the subject and private reason and sends the request.

2

Join the queue

The request is recorded immediately and remains visible until the scheduler reviews it.

3

Review and schedule

The Patron or Matron assigns an available date, prep, and Smart Class room.

4

Notify and publish

The requester receives an update, and the approved session appears on the public timetable.

User guide

Clear responsibilities for every role

Teacher

  • Selects a class and enters the subject and private reason.
  • Receives high priority in the pending queue.
  • Can edit or cancel a request while it is pending.
  • Receives notifications when the request changes.

Class Monitor

  • Submits a request for the assigned class.
  • Selects the responsible Teacher.
  • Tracks pending and previous requests.
  • Receives updates after approval, rejection, or cancellation.

Patron or Matron

  • Reviews the organized pending queue.
  • Chooses the date, prep, and available room.
  • Rejects requests when necessary.
  • Blocks unavailable rooms, slots, or full days.

Administrator

  • Creates and manages user accounts.
  • Maintains classes and Smart Class rooms.
  • Reviews reports and audit history.
  • Can reschedule or cancel approved bookings.

Frequently asked questions

What requesters and visitors need to know

Can a requester choose the Smart Class room and prep?

No. Teachers and Class Monitors submit the request without choosing a date, prep, or room. The Patron or Matron assigns those details during approval.

What happens after a request is submitted?

It enters the pending queue. Teacher requests receive high priority, and the Patron or Matron reviews each request before scheduling or rejection.

Can the public see the private reason for a request?

No. The public timetable shows only the prep, room, class, and Teacher for approved current-day sessions.

What happens when a Smart Class is unavailable?

The Patron or Matron or Administrator can block an exact slot, one room for a day, or the full day. A block cannot overwrite an existing scheduled booking.
